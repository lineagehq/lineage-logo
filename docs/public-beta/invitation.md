Hello,

prerelease, or another install source.

1. [Seatify public-beta quickstart](https://github.com/lineagehq/lineage-logo/blob/main/docs/public-beta/seatify-quickstart.md)

Complete the six protocol milestones in one independent attempt. Generate both
receipt identifiers locally exactly as the protocol directs; they distinguish
receipts, not humans. The maintainer cannot coach, hint, take control, answer
step-by-step questions, or repair your environment during the attempt. Use only
the shipped instructions and normal product help.

Return only the schema-valid controlled receipt JSON through the separately
supplied owner-approved private authenticated-sender intake. Send no free text,
feedback, credentials, tokens, private paths, SVG contents, screenshots, traces,
browser/session data, personal data, or attachment. If validation fails or no
approved intake instructions are supplied, transmit nothing.

The intake is open for at most 14 days. Sender distinctness is compared only
transiently, at most one passing receipt counts per sender, project-controlled
source payloads are removed within 24 hours, and no identity linkage or identity mapping is retained.
This makes no provider deletion or anonymity claim. There is no confidential
vulnerability channel, SLA, or confidential handling; do not send vulnerability
details through this walkthrough.

Thank you for considering the walkthrough.
