Open your existing logo

Already have an SVG you like? Open it in Lineage Logo, make precise adjustments, and save a new version for you or your agent to continue from.

npx lineage-logo launch --workspace /absolute/path/to/logos

Requires Node.js 22+ on macOS or Linux. Place your SVG in a concepts/ subfolder, pass its parent folder as the workspace, and keep the terminal running while you edit. Save a new iteration, then give your agent that exact file.
