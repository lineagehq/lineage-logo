# Repository instructions

## Local development URLs

- Always present and open local development servers on a descriptive `.localhost` subdomain, such as `seatify.localhost` or `lineage-logo.localhost`.
- Do not use raw `localhost` or `127.0.0.1` as the user-facing editor URL.
- Configure the local host, proxy, and allowed origin as needed, then verify the application and its live connections from the subdomain before handing it off.
